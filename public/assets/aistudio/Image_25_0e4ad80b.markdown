## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-precision, institutional visual language for the Brazilian Marine Corps training and doctrine command (CTDDCFN - Comando do Treinamento e do Desenvolvimento Doutrinário do Corpo de Fuzileiros Navais). The visual identity bridges naval military rigor, technical doctrine stewardship, and forward-looking digital capability.

The aesthetic fuses **Corporate / Modern** discipline with **High-Contrast Tactical Engineering**. Key characteristics:
- **Military Rigor & Purpose:** Every line, grid coordinate, and icon serves direct operational clarity without superfluous decoration.
- **Naval Authority & Technological Edge:** Deep oceanic teals convey heritage and institutional authority, while tactical renewal greens inject high-visibility operational signaling for status indicators and active controls.
- **Technical Readability:** Dense layouts remain legible under stress through calibrated typography, structured hierarchy, and an integrated icon system designed for instant tactical comprehension.

## Colors

The color palette reflects naval maritime authority combined with technical telemetry:

- **Primary (`#002E32` / `#00383D` - Graphite Teal):** The foundational institutional tone. Anchors top-level headers, command navigation, primary buttons, structural cards, and high-priority labels.
- **Secondary / Accent (`#BAD435` / `#8FA619` - Renewal Green):** High-visibility kinetic signal. Applied to active doctrine flags, operational indicators, critical interactive states, and search anchors. Text rendered on `#BAD435` surfaces must strictly use `#002E32` or `#1C1C1C` for full contrast compliance.
- **Tertiary (`#1C1C1C` - Graphite Industrial):** Anchors technical data points, code identifiers, high-contrast borders, and deep body text.
- **Neutral & Canvas (`#F2F5F4` / `#F8FAFC`):** Balanced, low-glare cool surfaces engineered for extended operational screen time.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#D7DBDA`):** Low-contrast structural delimiters framing cards, table rows, and panel partitions.

## Typography

The dual-type hierarchy balances commanding naval authority with rapid technical scannability:

- **Headlines (Montserrat):** Robust geometric structure communicating military strength, operational stability, and institutional authority across portal landing views and section headings.
- **Body & Labels (Inter):** Highly legible neutral grotesque engineered for maximum clarity in doctrine manuals, training bulletins (BONO), technical charts, and complex operational dashboards.
- **Military Case Standard:** Operational badges, classification categories, and metadata flags use uppercase styling (`label-sm`) with wide tracking (`0.06em`) to mirror tactical naval signifiers and standard doctrinal markings.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored on an 8pt base grid with a 4pt micro-unit for dense data panels, icon bounding boxes, and compact indicators:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column fluid grid, 1.5rem (`24px`) gutters, and 3rem (`48px`) margins. Content maximum width is constrained to `1360px` to maintain strict optical balance.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout, 1.25rem (`20px`) gutters, and 2rem (`32px`) margins. Sidebar navigation collapses into an off-canvas drawer.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, 1rem (`16px`) gutters, and 1rem (`16px`) margins. Multi-column metric groups collapse into single-column vertical cards.
- **Modular Rhythm:** Spacing between doctrinal sections uses `space-xl` (`40px`), while internal component spacing conforms strictly to multiples of `space-sm` (`8px`) and `space-md` (`16px`).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system uses **structural containment and low-contrast architectural depth** rather than decorative diffuse blurs:

- **Surface 0 (Ground Canvas):** `#F2F5F4` (Cool Light Grey) or `#F8FAFC` across extended views.
- **Surface 1 (Modules & Doctrinal Cards):** `#FFFFFF` with a crisp 1px perimeter border in `#D7DBDA` and subtle directional support: `0 1px 3px rgba(0, 46, 50, 0.05)`.
- **Surface 2 (Flyouts, Menus & Dropdowns):** `#FFFFFF` with a defined containment shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 46, 50, 0.12)` and a 1px border in `rgba(0, 46, 50, 0.12)`.
- **Surface 3 (Command Overlays & Modals):** `#FFFFFF` surface centered over an institutional dark scrim: `rgba(0, 46, 50, 0.76)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`.
- **Tactical Classification Indicators:** Module cards, threat bulletins, and training announcements feature a 3px accent line on the top edge in `#002E32` (Institutional) or `#BAD435` (Active/Operational).

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Soft (`1`)** shape profile (base radius `0.25rem` / `4px`), preserving sharp military discipline and technical structure:

- **Base Components (Buttons, Form Inputs, Filter Chips, Tooltips):** `0.25rem` (`4px`) corners.
- **Containers (Cards, Dialogs, Data Grids):** `0.5rem` (`8px`) corners for disciplined containment.
- **Pill Exception (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for live telemetry chips, security classification tags (e.g., "RESTRITO", "OSTENSIVO"), and circular icon containment buttons.

## Components

### Icon System & Technical Grid
The icon system is built on a strict vector grid with geometric precision reflecting naval operational instruments:
- **Base Grid & Sizing:**
  - `16x16 px`: Compact inline indicators, table actions, breadcrumb delimiters.
  - `20x20 px`: Primary interface controls, dropdown triggers, list metadata.
  - `24x24 px`: Standard grid for primary navigation, card headers, and toolbars.
  - `32x32 px`: Feature banners, tactical category hubs, and operational dashboards.
- **Stroke & Geometry:** Outline style with 1.5px stroke weight (16px and 20px) and 2px stroke weight (24px and 32px). Corner joints are softly radiused (0.5px to 1px). Bounding boxes enforce optical centering within a 2px inner safety margin.
- **Color Application Rules:**
  - *On Dark / Tactical Surfaces (`#002E32` or `#1C1C1C`):* Icons render in `#BAD435` (Renewal Green) for interactive and active items, or `#FFFFFF` for static navigational items.
  - *On Light Surfaces (`#FFFFFF` or `#F2F5F4`):* Icons render in `#002E32` (Graphite Teal) for institutional controls, `#1C1C1C` for content metadata, or `#8FA619` for status highlights.
